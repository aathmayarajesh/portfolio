About

From the lab to audit

In a chemistry lab, one missed variable can ruin a whole experiment. My BSc taught me to measure carefully, use controls, and record every step so someone else can check it.

Audit needs the same habits. Audit & Assurance was my strongest ACCA paper, with a score of 81. Risk, internal controls and the auditing standards came naturally to me.

I don't have work experience yet. I do have a strong technical base, good Excel skills, and the patience to trace every number back to its source.
