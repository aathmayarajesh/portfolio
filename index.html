About

A little about me

I'm Aathmaya, and I'm from Thrissur. When I finished my degree in early 2024, I realised I wanted a career in accounting, so I started ACCA that June. Switching fields felt like a big step at the time. Getting through nine papers in about two years, while picking up a whole new vocabulary, is the thing I'm quietly proudest of so far.

I'm drawn to audit because it's careful, honest work. People rely on financial statements to make real decisions, and someone has to check that the numbers mean what they say. I'd like to be good at that. I know there's a lot about the day-to-day job I can only learn by doing it, and I'm looking forward to learning from people who've been at it for years.

Away from my books, I dance and spend as much time as I can with my family. I'm happy to move anywhere in India for the right first role.
